# Personas

## Merchant Developer

Integrates Acme Pay into checkout and needs predictable idempotency behavior, clear error messages, and webhook replay safety.

## Support Agent

Investigates payment failures, refunds, merchant questions, duplicate emails, and payout delays. Needs audit logs and searchable payment intent state.

## Payments On-Call Engineer

Responds to provider outages, ledger mismatches, webhook replay incidents, and elevated decline rates. Needs runbooks, dashboards, and safe operational controls.

## Trust and Safety Reviewer

Reviews high-risk payments and merchants. Needs risk signals, manual override history, and reason codes.
