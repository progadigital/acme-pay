# Dashboards

## Payments Health

Shows payment success rate, provider timeouts, decline rate, p95 provider latency, and idempotency reuse.

## Webhook Health

Shows provider delivery volume, duplicate suppression, invalid signatures, and event processing latency.

## Ledger Reconciliation

Shows unmatched ledger totals, clearing-account balance, refund volume, and payout generation status.

## Risk Operations

Shows review queue size, blocked merchant count, false-positive overrides, and new-merchant high-value payment volume.
